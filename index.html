Independent project, 2026

Backtesting and risk framework for Indian equities

Four RSI and breakout trading rules, written down before any result was seen and tested on 454 Nifty 500 stocks from 2010 to 2026, with NSE costs, a locked out-of-sample period and 60 unit tests. Each rule was compared with the same trade placed on days it did not choose.

  • No rule beat its own control. A value rule earned +4.7% a trade; the same trade on any month earned +5.0%.
  • A sector filter lifted returns by 78%, and lifted the control just as much.
  • A ₹10 lakh portfolio fell −17.7% at worst against the Nifty's −38.4%, because only 29% of capital was invested.
Code and full write-up

How often each 99% VaR model was breached over 3,413 days of the Nifty 50. Historical and normal models broke almost twice as often as promised, in clusters. EGARCH with fat tails removed the clustering but overshot.

Academic project, 2026

Diabetes risk classification and Power BI dashboard

A logistic-regression classifier on 2,000 patient records, checked with stratified 5-fold cross-validation (AUC 0.972), plus a Power BI dashboard for exploring risk by age, BMI and glucose. The real question was where to set the screening cut-off.

The data turned out to be synthetic: a two-line rule, BMI at least 30 and glucose at least 140, matches 99.4% of labels and beats the model. I report that alongside the AUC.

Notebook and dashboard

Lowering the cut-off trades false alarms for missed cases. From 0.50 to 0.15, recall rises from 70% to 94% while false alarms go from 48 to 172.
